I teach by building

I teach and build in robotics, mobile robotics and industrial automation. I believe students learn best when they get to build, experiment, troubleshoot and solve real problems.

I hold a B.E. in Mechatronics and an M.E. in Industrial Automation and Robotics. My work combines teaching, practical training, project development, research and mentoring.

I am also growing my knowledge in artificial intelligence and research writing, and exploring where AI, robotics and automation meet.

How I mentor students

Mentoring is not giving answers. It is helping students think, explore, build, fail, learn and try again, until the project solves a real problem.

1

Discuss the idea

Understand the problem worth solving.

2

Choose the technology

Find what is technically possible and pick the right tools.

3

Build the prototype

Hands-on, with troubleshooting as part of learning.

4

Test and improve

Refine into a working product, then take it to hackathons, pitches and exhibitions.
